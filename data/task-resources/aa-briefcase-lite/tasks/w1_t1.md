# Task 1: Market Structure Map

## Objective

Create a single-page LaTeX document containing a TikZ diagram that maps the structure of the New Zealand egg market. This diagram will serve as a visual aid for understanding production systems and channels in the sector.

## Requirements

- Map the structure of the NZ egg market.
- Segment producers by production system.
- Segment by market channel.
- Visually indicate relative producer scale across the market.
- Clearly annotate the cage-ban transition flow.
- Depict the value chain end-to-end, with the target's positioning shown along it. Identify the relevant intermediaries.
- Surface the cross-stage commercial dynamics that matter for a producer in Aurora's position.
- Differentiate the named producers visually beyond volume share, reflecting the structural distinctions.

## Deliverable

**Filenames:** `market_overview.tex` and `market_overview.pdf`
